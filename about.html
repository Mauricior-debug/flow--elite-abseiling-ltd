Who we are

All technicians operate within the guidelines of the Industrial Rope Access Trade Association (IRATA), the international standard for rope access, with experience in rescue and work in safe and clean conditions.

Operatives also hold PTS, CSCS, chainsaw and confined-space qualifications. All equipment is certified and inspected regularly, in line with UK law.

The company maintains a management system that improves safety, productivity and efficiency, and that meets the requirements of each client.
