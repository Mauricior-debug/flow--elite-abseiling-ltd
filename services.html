01 · Rope access

Inspections and surveys

The condition of a structure is hard to judge from the ground. The inspection team examines concrete, paint, masonry and steel from the ropes, then reports on what they find and how the client may want to proceed.

  • Condition of concrete, paint, masonry and steel
  • Water ingress, corrosion and loose masonry
  • Notes and photographs for the later repair
  • Surveying for physical condition and structural stability
  • Repeat inspections so small faults are caught earlier

Regular inspections are how Elite Abseiling helps clients limit later repair, keep the building safer, and stay in line with the regulations that apply to it.

02 · Rope access

Cleaning and maintenance

From a window clean to a structural repair, the crew takes on maintenance in places that are difficult to reach, including the smaller tasks.

  • Window cleaning, including after construction or refurbishment
  • A recommended clean every 4 to 6 months to keep the glass fresh
  • Pressure washing for dirt and grease
  • Soft washing where pressure would be the wrong method: a low-pressure chemical spray for algae, mildew and fungi
  • Fabric canopies
  • Ducting: damaged sections, access hatches and internal cleaning
  • Gutters and downpipes: debris, blockages and replacement
  • Vegetation on buildings and inside gutters, including ivy and weeds
  • Industrial cleaning of dirt, limescale, vegetation, concrete and paint

Painting and protective coatings

Painting and protective coating for industrial and commercial buildings, including work that has to be done from the rope. Decorators on the crew hold NVQ level 2 diplomas in painting, with experience on sash windows and a working knowledge of the materials that keep a finish in place.

  • Steelwork painting and coatings
  • Masonry, timber and cladding coatings
  • Silos and vessels
  • Gutter lining systems
  • Balconies and balustrades
  • Waterproofing coatings and systems
  • Specialist treatments
  • Full building and structural painting
  • Difficult-access painting
