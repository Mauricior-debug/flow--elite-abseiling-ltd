Inspections and surveys

Whether inspecting concrete, paint, masonry or steel, the rope-access inspection team reports on the condition of a structure and how the client may want to proceed.

  • Visual inspections and reporting
  • Photographic and video surveys
  • Bridge and structural inspections
  • Condition surveys
  • Water ingress, corrosion and loose masonry

Regular inspections help limit later repair, keep the building safer, and stay in line with the regulations that apply to it.
